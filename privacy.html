Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Vivora is an early-stage product that helps people practice speaking a new language by calling a warm AI that only ever speaks your target language. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

Vivora is currently pre-launch. Today the only thing we collect from most people is the email address you give us to join the waitlist. The sections below also describe how the product will handle data once practice calls go live, so there are no surprises later.

1. Who we are

Vivora is built and operated by Mankirat Singh, based in Sweden. For any privacy question or request, email hello@vivora.app.

2. What we collect

  • Waitlist: your email address, and the language you tell us you want to learn.
  • Basic usage: anonymous, aggregated analytics about how the site is used (e.g. pages viewed), so we can improve it.
  • When practice calls launch: your account details, the languages and preferences you set, the audio of your practice calls and their transcripts, and the words you save.

3. How we use it

  • To run the waitlist and let you know when Vivora opens.
  • To decide which languages to build next, based on what people ask for.
  • Once live: to power your conversations, hand you the word when you freeze, save your words and recaps, and improve the experience.

4. Your voice and conversations

Your practice calls are processed so the AI can understand you and respond in your language. To do this we use trusted infrastructure and speech providers — including cloud hosting (Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure) and speech-to-text (Deepgram, AssemblyAI) — who process data on our behalf under contract and may not use it for their own purposes.

Your conversations are yours. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it to train third-party models. You will be able to control what is saved and to delete your saved words and recordings.

5. Sharing

We never sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers described above, who help us operate Vivora and are bound to protect it, or where we are legally required to.

6. Your rights

Because Vivora is run from the EU/EEA, you have rights under the GDPR, including the right to access, correct, export, or delete your data, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these, email hello@vivora.app and we will respond promptly.

7. Retention

We keep waitlist emails until you ask us to remove them or the waitlist closes. Account and conversation data will be kept only as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service, then deleted.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep yours safe and to limit who can access it.

9. Children

Vivora is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

10. Changes

If we update this policy, we will change the date above and, for material changes, let waitlist members know by email.
